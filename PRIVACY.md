# Privacy policy

**Open Captions collects nothing.** Everything it hears is turned into text on your phone, and
nothing you say, and nothing it writes, is sent anywhere.

*Last updated: 11 October 2026.*

## What stays on your phone

- **What it hears.** Speech goes straight from the microphone to the speech model running on the
  phone. Audio is not saved or uploaded.
- **Transcripts.** Conversations are saved on the phone so they can be read again later. You can
  delete them one at a time or all at once in Settings, and you can choose how long they're kept.
- **Settings and diagnostics.** Your settings, and a short technical log of each conversation
  (timings, sound levels, which microphone was used, no words), are kept on the phone.
- **Microphone test recordings.** If you run the microphone test in Settings, the short clips it
  records are kept on the phone until you delete them. They leave the phone only if you share them
  yourself.

## The one thing it downloads

The first time it's used, the app downloads its speech models, about 1.1 GB, from
[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co), where they're published. That's a normal file download: as
with any website, Hugging Face can see your phone's internet address. Nothing about you or your
conversations is sent. After the download, the app works with no internet connection at all.

## What isn't there

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- No third-party services, apart from the model download above.
